## ✂️ Editing tips

- Use the **hook part** (chorus/hook step) in the first 3 seconds; that's what makes people keep watching.
- Keep reels at **7–15 seconds** for loops, **30 seconds** for montages.
- Cut your clips on the **beat** (Instagram's *Sync to beat* / CapCut *Auto beat* does this for you).
- Mix it up: a Marathi track on a Hindi-heavy feed (or vice versa) stands out.
